# Project 2 evidence

Generated by our own engine for the Project 2 report (Exercise 9). Both scripts are rerunnable
and double as demo steps.

## One conflict: `conflict-evidence.sh`

Runs the two-writer test `ConflictIT#sqlRaceLeavesOneCommitAndOneErrorLineOnTwoThreads` (demo step 1),
copies the log lines that run appended into [conflict-race.log](conflict-race.log), loads them into
a `logs` table with `./engine`, and filters them with our own `SELECT`s (demo step 2). Output:
[conflict-queries.txt](conflict-queries.txt).

What the log shows:

| Thread | Statement | Base | What it logged |
|---|---|---|---|
| loser (pool thread) | `DELETE FROM trips WHERE city = 'Copenhagen'` | 1 | rewrote its partition into `trips-partition-1.bin`, then `ERROR conflict table=trips base=1 current=2` |
| winner (main thread) | `COPY trips FROM 'sorted-trips.csv'` | 1 | wrote `trips-partition-2.bin`, then `statement=COPY … version=2` |

Afterwards a `SELECT` reads version 2 with 16 rows: both COPYs, no deletion. The loser's
`trips-partition-1.bin` is an orphan that no catalog entry references, and number 1 is never reused.

## A DELETE in the catalog: `catalog-evidence.sh`

Loads `sorted-trips.csv` with 2 rows per partition (version 1), then runs
`DELETE FROM trips WHERE distance = 88` (version 2). Output:
[catalog-v1.json](catalog-v1.json), [catalog-v2.json](catalog-v2.json), their diff
[catalog-v1-v2.diff](catalog-v1-v2.diff), and the decision lines in
[catalog-delete.txt](catalog-delete.txt). Partition 1 (distance 88 and 95) is rewritten into
partition 4, which keeps only the 95 row. Partitions 0, 2 and 3 are carried over unchanged.
`version` goes from 1 to 2 and `nextPartition` from 4 to 5. The superseded `trips-partition-1.bin`
stays on disk.
